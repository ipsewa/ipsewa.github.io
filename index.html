Why IP Protection Matters in Nepal

Starting a business in Nepal involves more than choosing a good name and creating a logo. To build a strong and trustworthy brand, business owners should consider intellectual property protection from the beginning. A trademark, patent, or industrial design registration can help protect the value created by founders, companies, and innovators.

Nepal's IP system is governed by the Patent, Design and Trademark Act 1965 (PDTA). The Department of Industry administers all IP applications, examinations, publications, and registration certificates. Understanding these protections early helps you avoid costly conflicts and establish strong legal rights.
