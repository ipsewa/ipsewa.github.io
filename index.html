About IPSEWA

Starting a business in Nepal involves more than choosing a good name and creating a logo. To build a strong and trustworthy brand, business owners should consider intellectual property protection from the beginning.

A trademark, patent, or industrial design registration can help protect the value created by founders, companies, and innovators. IPSEWA is dedicated to guiding businesses through the complexities of IP protection in Nepal.

Why Intellectual Property Matters

  • Brand Protection: Prevent others from using your brand identity without permission
  • Competitive Advantage: Gain exclusive rights to your innovations and creations
  • Business Value: Increase your company's valuation and market position
  • Legal Security: Have legal recourse against infringement and unauthorized use
  • Investor Confidence: Demonstrate clear ownership of valuable assets to potential investors
  • Market Trust: Build consumer confidence in your authentic brand
