Trademark Registration in Nepal: Protect Your Brand, Patent, and Product Design

Starting a business in Nepal involves more than choosing a good name and creating a logo. To build a strong and trustworthy brand, business owners should consider intellectual property protection from the beginning.

A trademark, patent, or industrial design registration can help protect the value created by founders, companies, and innovators.

Why trademark registration in Nepal matters

A trademark is a distinctive sign that identifies a business, product, or service. It may include a brand name, logo, symbol, word, slogan, or a combination of these elements. Trademark registration in Nepal gives business owners stronger legal protection and helps customers recognize the real source of goods or services.

Without trademark protection, another business may use a similar name or logo. This can create customer confusion and may force a company to spend time and money on rebranding. Registering a trademark early is a practical step for startups, e-commerce stores, restaurants, technology companies, manufacturers, and service businesses.

Start with a trademark search in Nepal

Before filing an application, it is important to perform a trademark search in Nepal. A trademark search helps identify whether a similar brand name, logo, or mark has already been published or registered.

A good trademark search considers more than an exact word match. Similar spelling, pronunciation, logo design, and related goods or services can all affect the result. Checking the correct trademark class is also important because registration applies to specific categories of goods and services.

  • Similar spelling
  • Pronunciation
  • Logo & visual similarity
  • Goods, services & class

Businesses can use IP Sewa to search published trademarks, explore trademark classes, and receive guidance before filing an application. This helps founders make better brand decisions before investing in marketing, packaging, websites, and domain names.

Patent registration for new inventions

Trademark registration protects a brand identity, while patent registration protects qualifying inventions and technical solutions. Entrepreneurs, engineers, researchers, and product developers may need patent registration when they create a new process, machine, technology, or useful improvement.

Patent registration in Nepal can support innovation by giving inventors a clearer legal basis for commercializing their work. Before disclosing an invention publicly, it is wise to obtain appropriate professional advice and understand the filing requirements. Learn about patent registration with IP Sewa.

Industrial design registration for product appearance

Industrial design registration protects the visual appearance of a product. This may include its shape, configuration, pattern, surface decoration, or ornamentation. It is useful for businesses that create distinctive packaging, consumer products, furniture, accessories, or product designs.

A trademark protects the business identity, a patent protects the invention, and industrial design registration protects the product’s visual appearance. Many businesses benefit from using more than one type of intellectual property protection. Explore industrial design registration in Nepal.

How do trademarks, patents, and designs differ?

A trademark identifies your brand. A patent protects a qualifying invention or technical solution. An industrial design protects a product’s visual appearance. Depending on your business, more than one type may be relevant.

Why search before filing a trademark?

A search helps you find similar published or registered marks before you invest in a name, logo, packaging, or marketing. It should consider spelling, pronunciation, visual similarity, and related goods or services. Explore IP Sewa’s search support.

Should I speak to a trademark lawyer in Nepal?

Professional support is especially useful for choosing classes, reviewing similar marks, answering objections, managing opposition, or meeting deadlines. IP Sewa’s registration service provides guidance from IP professionals.

Where is a trademark application filed in Nepal?

Trademark applications are filed with the Department of Industry. IP Sewa can help with preparation, class selection, filing support, and tracking the application.
