The process

How a trademark gets registered in Nepal

Six stages at the Department of Industry. Patents and designs follow the same shape with a 35-day opposition window.

  1. 1
    Before filing

    Search

    Check the register for anything confusingly similar in your class.

  2. 2
    Day 0

    File

    Mark, applicant details and NICE class go to the Department.

  3. 3
    Months

    Examination

    Formalities and conflicts with existing marks are checked.

  4. 4
    On acceptance

    Publication

    The mark is advertised in the Industrial Property Bulletin.

  5. 5
    90 days

    Opposition

    Anyone may object while the window is open.

  6. 6
    Every 7 years

    Registration

    Certificate issues; renew within 35 days of each expiry.

Questions

IP protection in Nepal

Who registers trademarks, patents and designs in Nepal?

The Department of Industry, under the Patent, Design and Trademark Act 2022 (1965). All three rights are filed there.

Is Nepal first-to-file?

Yes. Priority goes to whoever files first, not whoever used the name or invention first, so searching and filing early matters.

How long does each right last?

A trademark lasts 7 years and can be renewed any number of times. A patent lasts 7 years and can be renewed twice. An industrial design lasts 5 years and can be renewed twice.

Can foreign companies register in Nepal?

Yes. Foreign applicants file with the Department of Industry through a local agent, with a power of attorney and the supporting documents. Nepal is not a member of the Madrid System, so a filing must be made in Nepal directly.
